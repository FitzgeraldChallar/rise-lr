The Liberia Climate Change Trust Fund (LCCTF) operational manual is developed under the enhancing Climate Information Systems for Resilient development (Liberia CIS Project), funded by Green Climate Fund (GCF), African Development Bank (AFDB) and the Government of Liberia.

The 36-month regional project, led by UNEP under the Global Biodiversity Framework Fund (GBFF) was to mainstream biodiversity conservation into productive sectors by addressing unsustainable sand extraction in Liberia and the Gambia.

Supported the Climate Information System Project (CIS) and the ERVCCS projects to develop the climate equipment needs assessment for Liberia.

The (LFSP) was a multi-stakeholder initiative implemented by the Ministry of Mines and Energy (MME), in partnership with EMG Consulting Group Ltd., with support from the Swedish International Development Cooperation Agency (Sida) and the Society for the Conservation of Nature of Liberia (SCNL), to strengthen the capacity of artisanal and small-scale miners to adopt Forest-Smart Mining practices that promote environmentally sustainable mining, forest conservation, and climate resilience in Liberia.

Involved conducting geological and geotechnical investigations to support the feasibility assessment, engineering design, and safe development of a proposed mini-hydropower facility in Lofa County, Liberia.

As a major industrial player operating heavy equipment, power systems, and specialized facilities in Liberia, Mantrac required an expert, certified third-party appraisal of its real estate holdings and physical infrastructure. RISE Liberia evaluated the fair market value, structural integrity, and asset baseline of Mantrac's properties, leveraging its technical expertise and consulting competencies.
