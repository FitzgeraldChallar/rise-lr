Projects
  • Climate Change Trust Fund Operational Manual
  • Mainstreaming Biodiversity in Sand Production
  • Climate Equipment Needs Assessment for Liberia
  • The Liberia Forest Sector Project (LFSP)
  • The Kahia II Mini Hydropower Project

The Liberia Climate Change Trust Fund (LCCTF) operational manual is developed under the enhancing Climate Information Systems for Resilient development (Liberia CIS Project), funded by Green Climate Fund (GCF), African Development Bank (AFDB) and the Government of Liberia.

The 36-month regional project, led by UNEP under the Global Biodiversity Framework Fund (GBFF) was to mainstream biodiversity conservation into productive sectors by addressing unsustainable sand extraction in Liberia and the Gambia.

Supported the Climate Information System Project (CIS) and the ERVCCS projects to develop the climate equipment needs assessment for Liberia.

The (LFSP) was a multi-stakeholder initiative implemented by the Ministry of Mines and Energy (MME), in partnership with EMG Consulting Group Ltd., with support from the Swedish International Development Cooperation Agency (Sida) and the Society for the Conservation of Nature of Liberia (SCNL), to strengthen the capacity of artisanal and small-scale miners to adopt Forest-Smart Mining practices that promote environmentally sustainable mining, forest conservation, and climate resilience in Liberia.

Involved conducting geological and geotechnical investigations to support the feasibility assessment, engineering design, and safe development of a proposed mini-hydropower facility in Lofa County, Liberia.

We aspire to see a climate-resilient, environmentally sustainable, and inclusive Liberia.

(01)

Environmental Policy, Governance & Justice

Advancing inclusive environmental governance by shaping policies, supporting carbon market regulation, and advocating for environmental justice to ensure all communities, especially marginalized groups, have a voice in decision-making and resource management.

(02)

Research, Monitoring & Institutional Strengthening

Conducting environmental research, impact assessments, and data-driven monitoring to inform policy, while strengthening the capacity of government institutions and local organizations to effectively enforce environmental regulations.

(03)

Climate Resilience, Ecosystem Restoration & Agriculture

Designing and implementing climate adaptation projects, restoring degraded ecosystems, and promoting climate-smart agriculture practices that enhance food security and help communities withstand environmental challenges.

(04)

Geotechnical Engineering

Providing geotechnical and geological solutions for infrastructure development, alongside developing waste management systems and promoting renewable energy to support sustainable and resilient communities.

(05)

Waste Management

RISE delivers innovative and environmentally responsible waste-management solutions that reduce pollution, promote recycling and resource recovery, and transform waste into opportunities for healthier communities and a circular economy.

(06)

Sustainable Energy

RISE advances affordable, reliable, and climate-smart energy solutions that expand access to clean power, reduce dependence on fossil fuels, and support resilient communities and sustainable economic development.

(07)

Sustainable Architecture

RISE promotes resilient, resource-efficient, and climate-responsive architecture that combines modern innovation with local knowledge to create safe, functional, and environmentally sustainable spaces for present and future generations.
