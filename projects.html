PROJECT 04

The Liberia Forest Sector Project (LFSP)

This multi-stakeholder initiative worked to strengthen artisanal and small-scale miners’ capacity to adopt Forest-Smart Mining practices that support environmentally sustainable mining, forest conservation, and climate resilience. Partners included the Ministry of Mines and Energy, EMG Consulting Group, Sida, and the Society for the Conservation of Nature of Liberia.
