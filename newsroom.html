The Liberia Climate Change Trust Fund (LCCTF) operational manual was developed under the Liberia Climate Information Systems for Resilient Development project, funded by the Green Climate Fund, African Development Bank, and the Government of Liberia.

A 36-month regional project led by UNEP under the Global Biodiversity Framework Fund, addressing unsustainable sand extraction in Liberia and The Gambia.

Supported the Climate Information System and ERVCCS projects to develop Liberia's climate equipment needs assessment.

A multi-stakeholder initiative to strengthen artisanal and small-scale miners' capacity to adopt Forest-Smart Mining practices in Liberia.

Geological and geotechnical investigations supporting the feasibility assessment and engineering design of a proposed mini-hydropower facility in Lofa County.

As a major industrial player operating heavy equipment, power systems, and specialized facilities in Liberia, Mantrac required an expert, certified third-party appraisal of its real estate holdings and physical infrastructure. RISE Liberia evaluated the fair market value, structural integrity, and asset baseline of Mantrac's properties, leveraging its technical expertise and consulting competencies.

RISE Liberia Signs Property Valuation Agreement with Mantrac CAT

September 30, 2026

On September 30, 2026, the Resilience Institute for Sustainability and Environment Consulting Group signed a contract agreement with Mantrac CAT, Liberia to undertake a property valuation assignment. Our Founder and Technical Manager Pope Alexander G. Wrehyou signed on behalf of RISE, marking an important milestone in our commitment to providing professional, evidence-based consultancy services in Liberia.

We thank the dedicated RISE team for their hard work and commitment, and extend our sincere appreciation to Mantrac CAT, Liberia for trusting us with this assignment. We look forward to a productive working relationship and delivering a thorough assessment that supports informed decisions about their property.
